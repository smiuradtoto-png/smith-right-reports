03 First Impression Analysis
Trust Perception 42/100 Needs Improvement

Why this score:

The current landing page leads with technical capability and speed claims. While effective in Western markets, this approach may feel slightly assertive to Japanese users who typically build trust through calm, reassuring communication before engaging.

💡 Hint:

Adding visible company information, a soft introductory statement, and trust signals near the top of the page may meaningfully improve first-impression trust.
Japan Market Fit 55/100 Needs Improvement

Why this score:

The product has strong technical fit for Japan's DX needs, but the current presentation does not yet signal Japan-market awareness. There are no Japanese language elements, Japan-specific case studies, or cultural trust signals visible.

💡 Hint:

Even a small Japan-focused section with one Japanese customer story or a Japanese-language summary can significantly increase perceived Japan-market relevance.
CTA Naturalness 68/100 Moderate

Why this score:

The primary CTAs are functional but lean toward urgency-driven language. Japanese users often respond better to lower-commitment first steps before being asked to sign up or start a trial.

💡 Hint:

Consider adding an intermediate CTA like "Learn how it works" or "See case studies" before the signup prompt to match the Japanese preference for information-first decision making.
Service Comprehension 72/100 Moderate

Why this score:

The core value proposition is reasonably clear for technical users. However, non-technical Japanese decision makers may struggle to quickly understand what TableCheck does and why it matters for their business.

💡 Hint:

A plain-language section using a simple workflow example tailored to Japanese business contexts could improve comprehension for non-technical stakeholders.
Ad Approval Risk 28/100 High Risk

Why this score:

Several elements in the current messaging carry meaningful risk under Japan's Google and META ad review guidelines. Specific efficiency claims and bold comparison language are the primary concerns identified.

🚨 Priority Action:

Before running ads in Japan, a compliance review of all ad copy and landing page claims is strongly recommended.
